How a license works

  • Pay once for each major version. A license covers every release of that app with the same first number: a SiGENTEST 1 license covers every 1.x release. A new major version is a new purchase, and the version you have keeps its license. A license bought for version 0 of an app also covers its version 1.
  • Nothing is ever locked. Every feature works with or without a license. Without one, the About box and exported reports say "Student, teacher and non-pro use". With one, they say "Licensed to" and your name or your company's.
  • Works offline. Your license is a small signed file that the apps check on the Mac itself, with no server. A show Mac with no internet takes it by drag and drop. One file holds every app you have bought.
  • Versions you already have stay free. Versions of an app released before its first licensed version keep their free terms, for any use.
